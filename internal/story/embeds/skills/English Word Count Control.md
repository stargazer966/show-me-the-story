# English Word Count Control

Read the supplied target and range before drafting. Reserve space for the closing beat and divide the remaining budget among scenes in proportion to their dramatic weight. Output only the fiction.

When expanding, develop an existing choice, its consequence, or a character exchange. When trimming, remove repeated observations, exposition and dialogue tags before cutting essential action. Preserve point of view, scene order, and the chapter ending. Follow the application’s measured word count in revision feedback.
